About

¡Hola! I am a Machine Learning Engineer and Data Scientist with 5+ years across industry and research, currently completing an MSc in Mathematical Engineering (Statistical Learning track) at Politecnico di Milano on an Invest Your Talent in Italy government scholarship. Alongside my studies I work as an AI Engineer at IMHTP in Milan, designing multi-agent LLM pipelines and real-time computer-vision systems.

Before moving to Italy I was a Semi-Senior AI / Data Science Engineer at Mercado Libre, where I shipped probabilistic forecasting for inventory optimization and pioneered reinforcement-learning and contextual-bandit models for dynamic pricing. Earlier, I worked as a data-science consultant at Quantil, combining applied research with client projects, and as a research assistant at Universidad del Rosario's Tic Tank group, which led to a workshop paper at ICML 2022 (LatinX in AI).

My background bridges economics, applied mathematics, and software engineering. I am especially interested in sequential decision-making, agentic LLM systems, 3D computer vision, and making things fast on GPUs.
